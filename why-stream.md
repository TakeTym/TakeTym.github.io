# Why Stream?

*By Katyonka & Sami*

---

## Why Stream?
- **It's fun!** You play games anyway - why not share it?
- **Community** - You meet AMAZING people. Real friendships that cross online/offline.

---

## Important Steps

### Just Start

- Don't wait until you're "ready."
- You play games anyway - hit "go live"!

### Build Real Relationships
- Go to OTHER streams first
- Chat, be genuine, make friends
- They'll start coming to YOUR stream
- Your community becomes THEIR community

### Support Others

- Raid smaller streamers & raid back those who raid you
- Lurking on their streams helps them
- Prime Sub for free, Follow, Sub, Gift Subs all help
- Shout out to your followers and other streamers

### Create Your Own Magic

- Custom channel point rewards (Chrono Crystals!)
- Inside jokes (like "Beer Me!")
- Let viewers PARTICIPATE in your stream

### Remember Why You Started

- It's not all about the money
- The friendships are worth more
